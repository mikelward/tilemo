                                Menu bar

                                While it's kept hidden, the pointer stops one point short of the top edge over the bar, so macOS's menu bar doesn't slide over it; the Apple logo at the bar's left opens the Apple menu. Off, the menu bar shows at the top edge as usual, and top hot corners work again.

                                Notifications

                                A Do Not Disturb schedule, shared with tide. It needs the Do Not Disturb shortcuts from Setup. A schedule that ends before it starts runs overnight.
