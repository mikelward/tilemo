Clocks

    A label shows on the bar: any text, nothing for just the time, or abbr for the zone's abbreviation. Enter sets it. The bar shows local last, after these.

    Muted

    Apps and sites that are never marked for attention. Mute one with Cmd+I, or a right-click, on its row in the switcher's Windows or Attention tab. macOS still shows their banners: turn those off in Notifications settings.
